---
title: "How long until I see data?"
description: "Seconds. Load your own site in another tab and the visitor count should move before you switch back."
category: "getting-started"
weight: 30
---

Seconds, not hours.

Open your site in another tab, then look at the live visitors pill in the top bar of the dashboard. It counts everyone seen in the last 30 minutes, and it refreshes every 30 seconds. Your own visit should show up in about that long.

The reports underneath work on the same data but they're not all instant in the same way. Realtime reads straight from raw events and refreshes every 30 seconds. The main dashboard reads from pre-built roll-ups, which is why 28 days of top pages comes back in about a tenth of a second instead of a couple of seconds — and it means a brand-new event can take a moment to appear in a card that's summarising a month.

If you've just added the site, the onboarding screen watches for the first event and tells you the moment it lands. You don't have to sit there; it's also fine to close the tab and check tomorrow.

What won't appear is history. Feasible starts counting the day you install it. Yesterday didn't happen as far as this site is concerned — unless you [import it from Google Analytics](/help/can-i-import-my-google-analytics-history/) or [from CSVs](/help/can-i-import-a-csv/).

Nothing after five minutes? Don't wait it out. Something is wrong and the [health panel will name it](/help/why-dont-i-see-any-data-yet/).
