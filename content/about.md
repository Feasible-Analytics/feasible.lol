[Cloudmanic Labs](https://cloudmanic.com?utm_source=feasible.lol) has shipped software for more than twenty years. **Spicer
Matthews** writes the code, answers support, and signs the invoices. That's the
org chart.

We make **[Harbor](https://harbor.my)**, a private notes app, and **Feasible**,
website analytics on one page. We built both because we wanted to buy them.

## How we make money

Feasible costs $9.99 a month or $99 a year. That's how we make money. No ads. No
investors. No data sales. We don't share your data or train on it.

The software is cheap to run, so [the price is cheap too](/why-feasible/).

## What "small" buys you

The person answering support can fix the code. There's no phone tree, account
manager, or sales quota.

There's also no 24-hour phone line, SOC 2 report, ISO 27001 certificate, or
third-party penetration test. If your company requires one, Feasible isn't a
fit. [See what we do have](/security/).

Support runs on Pacific time. Most email gets an answer in a day or two. [Here's
how support works](/support/).

## The obvious question

What happens to your data if we disappear?

Feasible is [open source](/open-source-web-analytics/). You can run the same
product yourself. You can also export every raw event as CSV, even after you
stop paying. Leaving shouldn't be a project.
