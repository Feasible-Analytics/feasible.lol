There is one plan. There isn't a starter tier that can't do funnels, a growth tier
that adds three seats, or a business tier that finally gives you the API. Everything

## What counts, and what doesn't

We count pageviews. That's the only number on your account that has a ceiling, and
the ceiling is a million a month across every site you track.

Everything else is unlimited and never counted:

- **Custom events** — as many as you want, firing as often as you want.
- **Goals, funnels and custom properties** — including the properties attached to
  every event.
- **Sites** — one account, as many domains as you like.
- **Team members** — five roles plus per-site guests, no per-seat charge.
- **Engagement pings** — the time-on-page and scroll-depth heartbeats the script
  sends never touch your limit.

This matters more than the headline price, because the industry does not agree on
what a "pageview" is. Some vendors bill for a broader unit, and the multiplier is
not always small:

| Vendor | What they bill for | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Feasible** | **Pageviews. Events and properties are unlimited and never counted.** | — |
| Plausible | Pageviews + custom events | [plausible.io](https://plausible.io/#pricing) |
| Fathom | Pageviews + custom events | [usefathom.com](https://usefathom.com/pricing) |
| Simple Analytics | Datapoints — pageviews, events, and **each stored event property** | [simpleanalytics.com](https://www.simpleanalytics.com/pricing) |
| Matomo Cloud | Hits — pageviews, events, downloads, outbound clicks and site searches | [matomo.org](https://matomo.org/faq/general/what-is-a-hit/) |
| Umami Cloud | Events — pageviews, custom events, and **each stored event property** | [umami.is](https://umami.is/pricing) |
| Pirsch | Pageviews + custom events + 10% of session-extension events | [pirsch.io](https://pirsch.io/pricing) |

Read the second column before you compare the first. None of this is a trick — every
one of those units is published and documented — but it means the tier you need is
often bigger than your traffic looks.

Here's the same shop on three meters. Say you do 400,000 pageviews a month, you track
outbound clicks and PDF downloads, and every purchase carries five properties: order
value, currency, plan, coupon and country.

- **Counting pageviews**, that's 400,000. The events and the properties are free.
- **On a datapoint meter** where each stored property counts, one purchase with five
  properties bills as six datapoints instead of one.
- **On a hit meter**, every outbound click and every download is its own hit on top
  of the pageview, so the number you're billed on isn't the number in your traffic
  report.

You end up shopping for a tier based on a number you can't easily predict. We'd
rather you shopped on the number you already know.

All of the above checked on their published pricing pages, September 3, 2026.

## Why it costs this little

Because counting pageviews is cheap, and we built the thing to match.

A stored event is about 210 bytes, all in. A million pageviews a month for a whole
year is a 294 MB database — you could email it. One process accepts around 6,000
events a second and takes about 13 microseconds to say yes to one. The minimum
server is one CPU core, 512 MB of RAM and a gigabyte of disk.

There's no cluster underneath this. One Go binary, one SQLite file per account, no
Postgres, no ClickHouse, no Redis, no queue, no Docker. Nothing is running that we'd
have to charge you for. [The whole thing is open source](/open-source/) if you'd
rather check that claim than take it.

## Over a million pageviews {#enterprise}

Above a million pageviews a month, or if you need data kept longer than five years,
email **[sales@feasible.lol](mailto:sales@feasible.lol)** and tell us roughly how
much traffic you have. There's no published enterprise ladder because there's no
enterprise product — it's the same software, and the conversation is about what the
volume actually costs to run.

Nothing about that conversation is urgent. Here's exactly what happens if you sail
past a million without talking to anyone:

1. We email you at 70%, 85% and 100% of the limit.
2. One full month over costs nothing and changes nothing.
3. Two complete months over in a row, and we email asking you to reply within 14 days.
4. No reply after that, and the dashboard locks.

**Collection never stops.** Not at the limit, not at the lock. Nothing is throttled,
nothing is sampled away, nothing is deleted, and there is no overage charge — that
tier doesn't exist in the code. Export keeps working the whole time. Reply to the
email, or drop back under the limit, and the dashboard comes back.

## The trial, refunds and cancelling

The trial is **30 days and takes no credit card**. There's no Stripe customer until
somebody actually pays, so there's nothing to forget to cancel.

**Refunds.** A yearly plan is refunded pro rata within 30 days of purchase, on
request — no form, and nobody will try to talk you out of it. Monthly isn't refunded;
cancelling stops the next charge and there's at most one month at stake.

**Cancelling** happens in your own billing portal, any time, without emailing us
first. Switching monthly to yearly prorates immediately. Switching back to monthly
takes effect at renewal.

fifteen seconds.

changes nothing. Two complete months over in a row and we email asking you to reply
within 14 days. If nobody replies, the dashboard locks — but collection never stops,

under, or reply to the email, and it unlocks immediately.
